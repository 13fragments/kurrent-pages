STEP 1. Give every span candidate exactly one label:

- "perception_error" (reading error): the program misread letters that are in the scan; the letters look similar. Example: the scan has "der Schmied Johann Kranz", the program writes "der Schmid Johanna Kranich". You can tell which place in the scan the program tried to read. - "convention_error" (only the spelling): the program expands an abbreviation, uses modern spelling, or sets characters differently from the scan. Extra table rules or a different text layout also count here.
- "order_layout_error" (stands elsewhere): the text is in the scan, but at another place or in another order.
- "fluent_fabrication" (invented): the text is readable, but nothing in the scan at that place could be read like this, or the text has no relation to the letters in the scan. The program's own sentences, such as "Here is the transcription ...", are invented. Example: the scan ends with "gegeben zu Freiberg"; the program continues "im Beisein der ehrbaren Zeugen und des Herrn Pfarrers", and nothing stands there in the scan.

- "gt_or_image_uncertain" (don't know): illegible, or you cannot decide.
The rule between the two that matter most: if you can tell which place in the scan the program tried to read, it is a reading error, however bad. Choose "fluent_fabrication" only when nothing in the scan stands there, or the text does not fit the letters in the scan at all.
