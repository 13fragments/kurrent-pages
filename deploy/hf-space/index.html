Kurrent Pages Leaderboard

Results of the Kurrent Pages Benchmark v1.0. The benchmark has 53 held-out pages of German handwriting and print from 1565 to 1948. Each engine reads the page image and returns the page text. We score that text against a private ground truth.

Metrics

CER
Normalized character error rate. Lower is better.
Entity recall
Share of the persons, places, and dates in the ground truth that appear in the output.
Seg-fail
Share of pages with a CER above 0.75 or an empty output.
Hard strata
The 33 pages in the hard strata.
Fabrication flags
Pages where long insertions are more than 10% of the text. A script sets this flag. Nobody has reviewed the flagged pages.

We publish aggregates only. Page scores, engine outputs, and the ground truth stay private. The benchmark protocol on GitHub has the full definitions.
