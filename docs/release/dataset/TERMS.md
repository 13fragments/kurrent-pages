# Access terms: Kurrent Pages Benchmark v1.0 page images

These images are the held-out test pages of the Kurrent Pages Benchmark v1.0. The benchmark
works only while no model trains on them and no text layer of them exists in
public. You get access on these conditions:

1. **Evaluation only.** Use the images to run an engine and submit its outputs
   as described in `SUBMITTING.md` of the benchmark repository, or to evaluate
   an engine for your own internal assessment.
2. **No training.** Do not use the images, or any text derived from them, to
   train, fine-tune, distill, or select a model or a model checkpoint.
3. **No redistribution.** Do not republish the images as a set, add them to a
   dataset, or upload them to a service that may train on them. Fetch each
   image from its source record if you need it for another purpose.
4. **No published text.** Do not publish transcriptions, engine outputs, or
   per-page scores of these pages. Aggregates over several pages are fine.
5. **Delete on request.** Delete your copy when the maintainer withdraws a
   benchmark version.

These terms are a condition of access through this repository. They do not
change the licence of any image: each image keeps the licence of its source
record (CC0, Public Domain Mark, or CC BY 4.0; see `metadata.csv`).
